Grays Bay road and port: Arctic investment readiness

Geospatial data and maps to support investment decisions for the Grays Bay Road and Port project.

Use case question

How can geospatial intelligence support investment decisions for the Grays Bay Road and Port project by identifying existing capacity, development opportunities, infrastructure gaps and potential risks?

About this use case

The proposed Grays Bay Road and Port project could improve access to critical minerals, transportation corridors and economic opportunities in Canada’s North. GEO.ca brings together authoritative geospatial information to help decision-makers assess investment readiness across four key themes:

Energy

Infrastructure

Housing

Workforce

Energy investment in the North

Key geospatial layers

  • Remote Communities Energy Database (2025)
  • Principal Mineral Areas, Producing Mines, and Oil and Gas Fields (900A)
  • Top 100 Exploration Projects
  • Indigenous Mining Agreements
  • Critical minerals advanced projects, mines and processing facilities in Canada
  • Renewable Energy Power Plants, 1 MW or more – North American Cooperation on Energy Information
  • Utilities and Communications (NT)
Explore layers as a map

Data gaps

  • Prediction modelling around energy use and potential, such as:
    • Energy demand forecasts
    • Emission projections
  • Reliable, nationwide datasets for energy infrastructure networks (e.g., pipelines and transmission lines) are not consistently available. Existing public data are often generalized, incomplete, or outdated, due to security, privacy, or proprietary data restrictions.

Investment insight

Determine whether sufficient energy resources exist to support regional growth and future industrial development. 

Infrastructure in Grays Bay

Key geospatial layers

  • Transportation 
  • Canadian Anchorages and Anchorage Areas 
  • NAV CANADA Airports 
  • Canadian Hydrographic Service Non-Navigation (NONNA) Bathymetric Data 
  • Northern Major Projects 
  • Projects and Transformative Strategies Referred to the Major Projects Office 
Explore layers as a map

Data gaps

Many of the data gaps are detailed information on remote, indigenous and privately owned and managed infrastructure, such as:  

  • Complete road information, such as surface materials, road conditions, traffic volumes and maintenance schedules, winter road accessibility, open and closure dates 
  • Port and anchorage capacities, airports and aerodromes 
  • Detailed infrastructure data for remote communities, Indigenous communities and Indigenous owned or operated infrastructure 
  • Infrastructure mapping on existing mining or resource extraction sites 

Investment insight

Assess regional connectivity, logistics requirements and infrastructure gaps that may affect development costs.

Housing in Grays Bay

Key geospatial layers

  • Inuit Communities 
  • First Nation Locations 
  • Community Well-Being Index 
  • Aboriginal Lands of Canada Legislative Boundaries 
  • Average value of dwelling (dollars) by census subdivision 2016 
  • Regional and Community Vitality Index

  • Complete road information, such as surface materials, road conditions, traffic volumes and maintenance schedules, winter road accessibility, open and closure dates 
  • Port and anchorage capacities, airports and aerodromes 
  • Detailed infrastructure data for remote communities, Indigenous communities and Indigenous owned or operated infrastructure

Workforce investment in the North

Key geospatial layers

  • 2016 Population Ecumene by Census Subdivision 
  • Indigenous Population of Canada (2016) 
  • Ratio of children and seniors to working-age populations (0 to 14 and 65 and over versus 15 to 64) by census subdivision, 2016 
  • Labor Force Distribution (LFD) for Natural Resource Sectors in Canada 2001-2021 
  • Proportion of Individuals with at Least a University Bachelor’s Degree in Official Language Minority Communities (Demographics) 
Explore layers as a map

Data gaps

  • Current geospatial datasets for statistics on population, labor force, unemployment, education etc. (i.e. post 2016 census)
  • Service availability and capacity information such as health services, education services, and employment services.

Investment insight

Evaluate workforce availability, recruitment challenges and opportunities for local participation in economic development.

GEO.ca provides access to authoritative geospatial information that helps decision makers understand regional conditions, identify opportunities, evaluate constraints and support evidence-based investment decisions for major projects in Canada’s North.
